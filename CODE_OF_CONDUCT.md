# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, appearance, background, experience level, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to feedback.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.
- Keep discussions relevant, inclusive, and professional.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, discrimination, intimidation, sexualized attention, publishing private information without consent, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation for conduct that violates this policy. Reports should be sent privately to the repository maintainer(s) through the contact route listed in the GitHub repository profile or `SECURITY.md`. Please include relevant links and a concise description. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant. The project applies the expectations above to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, code reviews, and community channels.
